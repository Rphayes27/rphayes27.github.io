About The Threat of Extinction

The Threat of Extinction is an educational website dedicated to endangered animals and the challenges threatening their survival. This website features mammals, birds, reptiles, and amphibians from around the world. Each featured species has its own page containing information about the animal, its habitat, population, threats, and conservation. The goal of this website is to provide information about endangered wildlife while bringing attention to species that are at risk of disappearing.

What Is Extinction?

Extinction occurs when no living members of a species remain anywhere in the world. Once a species becomes extinct, it is permanently lost. Species can become threatened for many different reasons. Habitat destruction, pollution, climate change, hunting, disease, invasive species, and other environmental changes can all cause animal populations to decline. Protecting endangered species can help preserve biodiversity and maintain healthy ecosystems for future generations.
