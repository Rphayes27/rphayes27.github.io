About The Threat of Extinction

The Threat of Extinction is an educational website dedicated to endangered animals and the challenges threatening their survival.

This website features mammals, birds, reptiles, and amphibians from around the world. Each featured species has its own page containing information about the animal, its habitat, population, threats, and conservation.

The goal of this website is to provide information about endangered wildlife while bringing attention to species that are at risk of disappearing.

What Is Extinction?

Extinction occurs when there are no living members of a species remaining anywhere in the world. Once a species becomes extinct, it is permanently lost.

Species can become endangered for many reasons, including habitat destruction, pollution, climate change, hunting, disease, invasive species, and other environmental changes.

Protecting endangered species is important because every species plays a role in its ecosystem. Conservation can help protect wildlife and preserve biodiversity for future generations.
