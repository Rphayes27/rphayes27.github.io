About This Website

The Threat of Extinction is an educational website created to bring attention to endangered animals from around the world.

Throughout this website, you can explore species belonging to several different animal groups, including mammals, birds, reptiles, and amphibians.

Each animal page provides information about the species, including its appearance, habitat, population, threats, and conservation.

What Is Extinction?

Extinction occurs when a species no longer has any living members remaining anywhere in the world. Once a species becomes extinct, it is permanently lost.

Animals may become threatened by habitat destruction, climate change, pollution, hunting, invasive species, disease, and other environmental pressures.

Learning about endangered animals and the problems affecting them is an important part of understanding how wildlife can be protected for future generations.
