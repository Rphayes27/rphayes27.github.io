Endangered Animals and The Threat of Extinction

Topic Description

Extinction is defined as the complete state or process of something dying out, vanishing, or no longer existing. There are many animals facing extinction in the world as the environment continuously gets worse. Factors like natural disasters, pollution, climate change, and poaching all contribute to worsening species populations, and I want to draw attention to these issues.

There are many applications that give you brief overviews of endangered animals, oftentimes giving users short descriptions of the animals and the problems they face without going into a lot of detail. Websites such as the Animal Welfare Institute and Rainforest Alliance are examples of pages that, I feel, do not give the user enough information to care about the endangered animals being represented. I want my application to give you a bit more information on the animal so as to give users a better understanding of why exactly the animal is facing extinction and why we should care about their loss.

This topic choice stemmed from "Biology and Ecosystems." I've always been interested in animals. I love them and it is extremely hard for me to find one that I don't think is cute in some way.

App Description

My app will give you detailed information about 4 different animal groups, each with their own 4 distinct species. Each of these animals will have their own section with at least 3 images showcasing their beauty and environment, a description of the animal and where they are located, information on how the animal contributes to the environment, and in depth information about what issues are contributing to their extinction or endangerment.

First Choice: Animal Groups

The user will first choose one of four different animal groups. The four groups are mammals, birds, reptiles, and amphibians.

Second Choice: Specific Animals

After choosing an animal group, the user will be able to select one of four specific animals within that group. The second choice will depend on the first choice.

Mammals

  • Black Rhinos
  • Saolas
  • Red Wolf
  • Pangolins

Birds

  • Kakapo
  • Kagu
  • California Condors
  • Red Crowned Amazon

Reptiles

  • Blue Iguana
  • Central American River Turtle
  • Dary's Burrowing Snake
  • Red Bellied Racer

Amphibians

  • Axolotl
  • Lemur Leaf Frogs
  • Golden Toads
  • Olms

Information About Each Animal

After selecting an animal, the user will be able to view a section dedicated to that species. Each section will contain at least 3 images showcasing the animal and its environment. It will also include a description of the animal, where it is located, how it contributes to the environment, and in-depth information about the issues contributing to its extinction or endangerment.
