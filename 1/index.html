Izuku Midoriya - Hero name: Deku

Izuku Midoriya is the main protagonist of the anime My Hero Academia. In a world of "quirks", or superpowers, this boy was born without one. After being told his whole life that he would never make it as a pro-hero, he inherits the quirk of the worlds number one hero and enrolls in a prestigous hero course at U.A.. The story follows him as he faces villians with his class and, at the end of the story, he gives up his beloved quirk to save the main villian. I like Deku because he's brave and selfless. He gave up his ultimate dream just to help someone society had deemed irreparable. He's also super powerful and his fight scenes are awesome.
